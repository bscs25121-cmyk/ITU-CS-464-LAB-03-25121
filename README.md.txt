Game 1: Toca Life World
 link: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tocaboca.tocalifeworld Genre: Simulation / Sandbox I played: 45 minutes; built 2 characters and decorated an apartment.

Mechanics:
Character Creator: Players select from hair, face, skin, and outfits to build up to 3 custom characters.
Item Drag and Drop: Any item in the world can be dragged into a character's hand or placed on tables.
Furniture Decorator: Players open a menu and drag furniture anywhere in a house.
Weekly Post Office Gifts: Every Thursday, a central gift box gives 1 free unique item.
Facial Expressions Wheel: Tapping a face opens a menu with 10 different facial expressions.
Carrying Items Across Rooms: Items held in a character's hand stay with them when moving to a new area.
Unlocking Map Locations: Extra buildings on the map are unlocked with real money purchases.
Dynamics:
Players spend extended time fine-tuning character appearances to reflect themselves or famous people.
Players set up physical scenes like picnics or parties by moving specific items into a single room.
Players act like interior designers and rearrange furniture repeatedly for a good look.
Players open the game once a week specifically to claim their free gift from the post office.
Players change character facial expressions to match the story they are acting out during play.
Players take items from shops and bring them back home to permanently keep or hoard.
Players choose which new places they want to unlock and expand into next.
Aesthetics:
Expression (Core): Making custom characters and designing rooms to express personal style.
Fantasy: Making up fun pretend stories with props and changing facial expressions.
Submission: Easy daily or weekly check-ins for free post office gifts.
Bartle Player Types:
Primary: Explorer (Interacting x World), because players focus on discovering how items move, carrying items across maps, and exploring unlocked locations.
Secondary: Achiever (Acting x World), because players like completing full room designs and collecting all free gifts.

Screenshots Captions:
Adding wood to the campfire to keep light up and stay alive.

Mechanics:
Campfire Fuel System: The fire loses heat over time, so adding wood increases light radius and safety.
Hunger System: The hunger meter drops over time, and at 0 hunger the player loses health until they eat.
Night Monster Raids: Enemies spawn every night and attack the campfire and players.
NPC Barter Trader: An NPC appears on set days to trade rare animal drops for upgraded tools.
Scrap Recycling: Metal junk from old buildings can be ground into crafting scrap.
Class Selection: Players pick roles like Lumberjack or Medic in the lobby for stat boosts.
Diamond Upgrades: Surviving nights awards Diamonds to permanently unlock new classes in the menu.
Dynamics:
Players collect wood during the day and rush back to feed the fire before dark.
Players split work between gathering berries or building farms so nobody starves.
Teammates gather at base before sunset, build walls, and fight off monsters together.
Players hunt specific animals quickly during early day cycles to get trades ready.
Players explore abandoned houses to loot heavy metal objects for base processing.
Players pick different roles during lobby setup so the team covers all work needed.
Players try to survive as many nights as possible to earn Diamonds for main menu unlocks.
Aesthetics:
Challenge (Core): Managing fire decay, hunger depletion, and night monster attacks under time pressure.
Fellowship: Working together with teammates using specialized roles to defend base.
Discovery: Exploring outer map areas to loot scrap metal and hunt rare animals.
Bartle Player Types:
Primary: Achiever (Acting x World), because the main goal is mastering survival systems, upgrading the fire, and reaching maximum nights.
Secondary: Killer (Acting x World and Players), because players focus on fighting off hostile enemy raids and protecting base.
